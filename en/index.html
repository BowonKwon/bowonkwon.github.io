Public materials compiled with generative AI

These are not finished books or settled conclusions but a record of experiments — things tried out, and material gathered and put together, with generative AI. Legal-language handbooks, AI handbooks, an evidence table for animal-signal research, and five data sites that count classical music, physical constants, the Joseon Veritable Records, pre-modern privileges and Korean literary collections, and three history sites built from papers, music charts and book publishing. Each release was checked against the originals and reviewed by an independent AI reviewer, but the work is still being revised and errors may remain. Anything wrong — a reading that departs from the original, a typo, a faulty gloss, a dead link — please report it via each repository's Issues.

An open data site that reads scores and performances as numbers. Publishing history · timeline

A History of Book Publishing

A history site that follows, from manuscripts to e-books, how many books appeared each year together with how they were counted. Each source gets its own line, and the years in which a source changed its counting are marked. The German, Dutch and incunabula union catalogs were recounted under rules fixed in advance; official tables for Korea, Japan, China and the United States were transcribed from the originals and checked against them.

Sillok Metrics

The Veritable Records of the Joseon Dynasty counted across 414,501 articles: 34 metrics, each with a reliability card saying how far to trust the number. Version 1.2 adds two series. One codes 13,337 astronomical articles from the Goryeosa astronomical treatise, the Goryeosa-jeolyo and the Seungjeongwon Ilgi with the Sillok's codebook and checks them against the same computed sky. The other splits the Ilgi's daily weather cell into tokens and compares it with rain-gauge and modern station records.

Munjip Metrics

The full XML text of the 1,249 collections of the Hanguk munjip chonggan, split into 637,203 works (one poem, one letter, one epitaph), counted in 23 metrics across four series: size, genre composition, relationship genres, authors and publication. Genre classes are the ITKC's own; parsing and linkage steps carry sampled precision. No text is reproduced; every work links to the ITKC database.
